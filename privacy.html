2. What Information We Collect

We collect the following information through this website:

  • Enquiry and quotation form: Name, organisation, work email address, phone number (optional), enquiry type, product of interest, quantity, and message content.
  • Telegram product enquiry: Name, organisation, email address, quantity, and message content submitted through the Telegram enquiry form on the website. These are transmitted to our Telegram bot.
  • Calendly consultation booking: Information provided during the booking process, including name and email address. Calendly processes this data under their own privacy policy.
  • Website hosting and access logs: The website is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may collect server access logs including IP addresses and browser user agent strings. See the Vercel Privacy Policy for details.

We do not use cookies for tracking or analytics by default. If analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your equipment enquiry or quotation request.
  • Provide information about products and services relevant to your enquiry.
  • Arrange consultations when requested.
  • Communicate with you about your order or enquiry.

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Formspree — processes contact and quotation form submissions and forwards them to our email address. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Calendly — manages consultation bookings. See the Calendly Privacy Notice.
  • Telegram — product enquiries submitted through the Telegram enquiry feature on this website are delivered to our Telegram bot. See Telegram's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — hosts this website. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — this website loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. Google's servers may receive your IP address when fonts are loaded. See Google's Privacy Policy.
