Our Services

Laboratory equipment supply, installation, training, and support for healthcare facilities across Nigeria.

What We Do

More Than Equipment Supply

Harles Lab Equipment is a laboratory equipment supplier based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. As an official Zybio distributor, we source and supply quality diagnostic instruments and laboratory equipment for hospitals, diagnostic centres, research institutions, and other healthcare facilities.

We understand that acquiring laboratory equipment is only part of the process. Our team provides installation, training, and technical support to help your laboratory get the most from its instruments.

Equipment Supply

We supply genuine laboratory diagnostic instruments from authorized manufacturers. Our primary range includes Zybio laboratory equipment, covering haematology, biochemistry, immunoassay, urinalysis, and coagulation systems. Contact us with your specifications to discuss availability and quotation.

Installation & Commissioning

Our team provides professional installation and commissioning of laboratory equipment at your facility. We ensure each instrument is correctly configured and operating before handover, giving your laboratory team confidence in its performance from day one.

Operator Training

We provide hands-on training for laboratory staff on the correct operation, daily maintenance, and troubleshooting of supplied instruments. Training is conducted at your facility to ensure your team can operate equipment confidently and safely.

Technical Support

After equipment delivery, our team remains available to assist with technical questions, operational guidance, and troubleshooting. Reach us by phone, email, or Telegram for responsive support.

Equipment Consultation

Not sure which equipment best suits your laboratory? Book a free consultation with our team. We will review your laboratory's testing requirements, workload, and budget to help you identify the most appropriate instruments.

Quotation & Procurement Support

We provide clear, itemized quotations to support your procurement and budget approval process. Our team will work with you to ensure the quotation meets your organization's requirements.
