Who We Are

Your Laboratory Equipment Partner in Port Harcourt

Harles Lab Equipment is a laboratory equipment supplier based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. We supply diagnostic instruments and laboratory equipment to hospitals, diagnostic centres, research institutions, and healthcare facilities.

As an official distributor of Zybio laboratory equipment, we provide genuine instruments supported by manufacturer documentation, installation services, and technical support.

Our focus is on helping laboratories acquire the right equipment for their testing requirements, with clear procurement support and responsive service.

Official Zybio Distributor

Harles Lab Equipment is an authorized distributor of Zybio laboratory equipment. Zybio is an in vitro diagnostics company that manufactures a range of clinical laboratory instruments including haematology analysers, biochemistry analysers, immunoassay systems, urinalysis equipment, and coagulation analysers.

As an authorized distributor, Harles Lab Equipment supplies genuine Zybio instruments with access to manufacturer documentation and support channels. Contact us to verify specific product availability and our current distributor status.
